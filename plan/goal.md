The Goal

* Use scraped information on over 500 businesses from the Batavia Chamber of Commerce  
  * Batavia\_businesses\_with\_summaries.json  
  * *Note, an agent was used to add a one sentence summary for each entity found in the scrape*  
* Add in \~1100 additional companies within Batavia from a FOIA request  
  * InspectionsExport-combined.csv  
* Use agents to research and build full company profiles for every business in the master list (chamber scrape plus the FOIA list).   
  * Business location and hours of operation  
  * Regular or upcoming events  
  * Contacts and any people associated with the business or org  
  * Products and services and pricing  
  * About the business  
  * Any and all reviews about the business or entity  
* Use agents and other means — like human, manual addition of additional entities into the master list:  
  * Missing businesses  
  * Missing organizations  
  * Additional places of interests, like parks, schools, school teams and clubs, non school clubs, churches, Boardwalk shops, regular farmers market stands   
* Use agents to find every possible event associated with every entity for a real-time, continuous up to date current and future events list  
* Find any high quality assets: images and videos for every business and org  
* Create a categorization and tagging definition that covers every business and organization within the master list  
* Categorize and tag every business and org in the list and continuously update those categories and tags  
* Create a list and definition of personas who would find different interests in how they would interact with the different businesses and orgs and the events and products and services offered by each of the businesses.   
* Create a dynamic mapping system which maps these organizations to the different personas  
* Create a landing page which allows a visitor to choose a persona  
  * From that persona, the visitor then sees important events they might be interested in. Important products or services they could be interested in and depending on the current season. They can listen to a weekly podcast that outlines those events, orgs, products and service highlights. They can subscribe to a weekly newsletter that delivers the same info to their inbox. It’s important that all events and interests are covered. For example, a young family that has children is also alerted to any events happening in their kid’s school as well as regular business or organization events.  
  * From that persona view, the visitor can click on any organization and visit a branded landing page that highlights everything about that business \- like a long scroll single page website. The information and how it’s written in tone is individual to that brand but also tied to the Batavia community as a whole. There is a consistency and universal tone in how the individual landing pages look and sound while also enabling full individual branding and voice for each entity.  
* Agent are going out daily and updating the information for all entities regularly  
* Finally, any of the businesses or entities can hire a local intern to audio and video interview the businesses, take pictures and capture deep dives of information that is then fed back into the system to build out their presence and landing page even more deeply and comprehensively. This is continuously done as well.  
* Businesses or entities will be able to bid for advertising positions in all the various interactions with the website, the newsletter and podcast.  
* Businesses or entities will be able to pay a small fee to continuously add or change information about themselves in the system via a simple chat interface. No standard content management system. Just a log in and then a chat interface where they chat any changes or additions which include adding photos and videos.  
* So that’s three investment opportunities.  
  * \#1: pay to edit your own information within the system  
  * \#2 pay to advertise at different points in the system, newsletter and podcasts (each persona gets a custom weekly podcast and newsletter)  
  * \#3 pay to have interns capture, produce and install content on your behalf
